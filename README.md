# Hospital-Dashboard
## 1. **Project Title**

**Healthcare Patient Waitlist Analytics Dashboard — Power BI**

---

## 2. **Project Overview**

This interactive Power BI dashboard analyzes multi-year patient waitlist data to help healthcare administrators monitor backlog, understand wait-time distribution, and identify operational bottlenecks across specialties and case types. The report enables data-driven decisions for improving patient flow, prioritization, and resource planning.

---

## 3. **Tech Stack**

* **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling and interactive visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation (ETL)
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI measures and time intelligence
* **Excel / CSV files** – Historical waitlist datasets

---

## 4. **Data Source**

* Hospital waitlist records from **2018 to 2021**
* Case types: **Outpatient, Day Case, Inpatient**
* Specialty-wise patient queues
* Monthly archived snapshots for trend analysis

---

## 5. **Core Features**

* KPI comparison of **Latest Month Waitlist vs Previous Year**
* Distribution of patients by **Case Type** using donut visualization
* Waitlist segmentation by **time bands** (0–3 months to 18+ months)
* Specialty-wise **Average vs Median** waitlist toggle
* Yearly trend analysis of Outpatient, Day Case, and Inpatient volumes
* Interactive filters for **Date Range, Case Type, and Specialty**
* Clear identification of long-wait patient groups for operational focus

---

## 6. **Key Business Questions Addressed**

This dashboard provides answers to important operational questions:

1. Which case type contributes the most to the overall waitlist?
2. How has the patient backlog evolved over the years?
3. Which specialties experience the highest wait times?
4. What proportion of patients are waiting longer than 12 months?
5. Are outpatient cases growing faster than other case types?
6. Which time band requires immediate intervention?
7. How does current performance compare to the previous year?
8. Where should hospital management prioritize resources to reduce delays?

---

## 7. **Business Value**

The dashboard supports healthcare decision-makers in identifying inefficiencies, prioritizing critical cases, and optimizing scheduling strategies. By transforming raw waitlist data into actionable insights, it contributes to improved service levels and better patient experience.
